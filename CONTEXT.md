# A Picture Show with Friends

Showtimes from six San Francisco indie theaters, gathered on one page. A
visitor looks through what is playing, stars the showings they want, and sends
that list to friends. This is the language of the front page.

## The two lists

**Browsing**:
Every upcoming showing at all six theaters, grouped by day. Where a visitor
looks for something to see.
_Avoid_: browse list, catalog, all showings, results, feed

**Wish List**:
The showings a visitor has starred, held in their own browser and nowhere
else. The list they send to friends.
_Avoid_: favorites, watchlist, saved, my list, "wishlist" as one word in prose

**Starring**:
Marking a showing for the Wish List by clicking its ★. The one action the
front page asks of a visitor.
_Avoid_: favoriting, saving, bookmarking, liking

**Pane**:
The region holding one of the two lists. There are exactly two, one per list,
and they hold the same showings — Browsing shows them all, the Wish List pane
shows the starred ones.
_Avoid_: column, panel, view, tab

**Info box**:
The introduction that occupies the Wish List pane while nothing is starred. It
is what a first-time visitor reads, and it disappears at the first star.
_Avoid_: empty state, intro, blurb, welcome text

## Desktop and mobile

The same two panes on both. What changes is how many a visitor can see at once.

**Desktop**:
Wide enough for both panes at once, side by side: the Wish List at the left,
Browsing at the right. A visitor stars a showing and watches it arrive in the
Wish List without going anywhere.
_Avoid_: wide, large screen, two-column mode

**Mobile**:
Narrow enough that only one pane fits, so the visitor sees one list at a time
and switches between them. Starring still works the same, but the result lands
in a list that is out of sight.
_Avoid_: phone, small screen, narrow, one-column mode

**Tab bar**:
The pair of labels a visitor taps to switch panes on mobile. It names both
lists, so on mobile the names are always on screen. It is not present on
desktop.
_Avoid_: tabs, switcher, toggle, segmented control
